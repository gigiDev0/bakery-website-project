✦ Our Beginnings ✦

From a Small Kitchen
to Sunyani's Heart

Golden Crumb Bakery was born in 2014 from a simple dream — to bring the warmth, comfort, and joy of freshly baked goods to the families of Sunyani. What started as a small home kitchen run by our founder, Maame Akua Boateng, has blossomed into one of the most beloved bakeries in the Bono Region.

Maame Akua learned to bake from her grandmother in the village of Wenchi, carrying down recipes steeped in tradition while adding her own contemporary flair. Her vision was always clear: use the best locally sourced ingredients, bake with heart, and serve with a smile.

Today, Golden Crumb is a team of passionate bakers, decorators, and customer service professionals united by one goal — to make every visit and every bite a memory worth cherishing.

✦ What We Stand For ✦

Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To craft premium baked goods using the finest locally sourced ingredients, and to serve the people of Sunyani and the Bono Region with exceptional taste, warmth, and consistent quality — every single day. We believe food has the power to bring people together, and our mission is to be the centrepiece of those shared moments.

🌟 Our Vision

To be Ghana's most beloved artisan bakery brand — one that is synonymous with quality, community, and the irresistible aroma of fresh-baked goodness. We envision a future where Golden Crumb is present in every Ghanaian celebration, morning routine, and family gathering from Sunyani to Accra and beyond.

✦ Our Journey ✦

A Decade of Golden Moments

2014
The First Loaf
Maame Akua bakes her first batch of bread from her home kitchen on Brong Avenue, Sunyani, and distributes them to neighbours. The response is overwhelming.
2016
Opening Our First Shop
Golden Crumb opens its first physical bakery in the Sunyani Central Market area. Within weeks, queues form daily before 7 AM.
2018
Custom Cakes & Events
We expand our offerings to include custom celebration cakes for weddings, birthdays and corporate events across the Bono Region.
2021
Award-Winning Bakery
Recognised as the Best Artisan Bakery in the Bono Region at the Ghana Food & Hospitality Awards, cementing our place as Sunyani's finest.
2024
Decade of Baking
We celebrate 10 years of Golden Crumb with a new flagship store, an expanded menu, and delivery services across Sunyani and surrounds.
